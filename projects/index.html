Real-time multimodal assistant on wearable devices (DARPA PTG)

A vision-language-audio assistant that sees what the wearer sees, listens, answers in speech and guides physical tasks step by step; demonstrated live at the DARPA PTG program review at MIT (Oct 2023) and evaluated by MIT Lincoln Laboratory.

  • Evaluated by MIT Lincoln Laboratory

  • Scale live on HoloLens and a helmet-mounted camera-and-microphone rig
